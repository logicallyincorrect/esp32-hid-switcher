03 / OUTPUTS

Outputs

Select a slot, then open that computer’s Bluetooth settings and pair with HID SWITCHER BLE. Repeat for the other computers. Slot order is left to right.

04 / SWITCHING

Choose how to switch

Shortcuts work immediately. Seamless edge switching is optional and uses the monitor arrangement below. Calibration is optional.

Seamless switching

Move past a screen edge to switch computers.

Turn on to switch at screen edges.

Start at 100%. Adjust the speed for all screens together; each screen can override it by a percentage.

Optional calibration

Move to the selected computer’s top-left corner, click and release. Wait for the bottom-right prompt, then move smoothly to that corner and click again. These clicks stay on the switcher.

Extra mouse movement past the edge before switching. Increase to avoid accidental switches.
